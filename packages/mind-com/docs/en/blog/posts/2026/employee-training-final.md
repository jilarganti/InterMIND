## How Real-Time AI Interpretation Works

Real-time AI interpretation technology uses artificial intelligence and machine learning to convert spoken language into text and translate it into the target language almost instantaneously. Modern systems achieve **97% accuracy** thanks to neural networks and deep learning.

_Source: ScienceDirect - AI Translation in Teaching_

<div class="technology-comparison">

<div class="solution-card">

#### 🎤 Speech Recognition (ASR)

Captures spoken language and converts it to text with up to **95% accuracy** for clear speech.

</div>

<div class="solution-card">

#### 🧠 Neural Translation (NMT)

Translates text while maintaining context. Modern models understand linguistic nuances.

</div>

<div class="solution-card">

#### 🔊 Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Converts translated text back into sound with natural intonation.

## Challenges and Limitations

<div class="penalty-analysis">

#### Technical Limitations and Solutions

**🎤 Accents and Speech Speed**  
AI may struggle with strong accents or fast speech.  
_Solution:_ Trainers speak clearly and at a moderate pace.

**📚 Industry Jargon**  
Specialized terminology may be translated inaccurately.  
_Solution:_ Prepare a glossary for participants to review in advance.

**🌐 Internet Dependency**  
Most solutions require stable connectivity.  
_Solution:_ Ensure quality Wi-Fi and backup channels for critical sessions.

**🌍 Cultural Sensitivity**  
AI may miss cultural context.  
_Solution:_ Trainers clarify culturally specific points.

</div>
